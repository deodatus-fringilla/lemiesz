## 1. Rejection of the Hegelian Dialectical Method (*Odrzucenie Dialektyki Heglowskiej*)

**Przymierze Lemiesza explicitly rejects Hegelian dialectics** (*thesis → antithesis → synthesis*).

1. **Why We Reject Hegel:**
   - Hegelian methodology treats truth as a fluid historical process in which contradictions are merged into an artificial *synthesis*. In political practice, this relativizes moral absolutes, subordinates the human person and family to the idol of the State, and allows controlled opposition to steer nations into pre-arranged conflicts.
   - Przymierze Lemiesza stands on **classical Christian realism, objective truth (*Veritas*), and the Law of Non-Contradiction**: a proposition is either true or false; a policy either serves the survival and flourishing of the nation or drains its lifeblood.
2. **Our Alternative — The Five Classical Methodologies (*Pięć Klasycznych Metodologii Dysputy*):**
   Instead of Hegelian synthesis, every debate, letter, and Shield rebuttal in Przymierze Lemiesza uses one or more of the following five classical methodologies, depending on context — with primary emphasis on the first two:

| # | Methodology (PL / LA / EN) | Core Structure & Application |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Dysputa Tomistyczna** (*Methodus Thomistica* / Thomistic Disputation) | Following St. Thomas Aquinas (*Summa Theologiae*): **(1) *Videtur quod* (Zarzut):** state the opponent's strongest objection fairly and accurately; **(2) *Sed contra* (Przeciw temu):** invoke the higher authority of Scripture, natural law, or binding treaty; **(3) *Respondeo* (Odpowiedź):** resolve the question through rigorous logical distinction. |
| **II** | **Rycerska i Pryncypialna Polemika** (Chivalrous & Principled Polemics) | *„Życzliwa, lecz pryncypialna polemika”*: charity and respect toward the interlocutor (*captatio benevolentiae*), combined with uncompromising fidelity to first principles. We never caricature an opponent with a strawman (*chochoł*); we honour common patriotic premises while refuting false conclusions. |
| **III** | **Apologetyka Klasyczna i Refutacja** (*Refutatio* / Classical Apologetics & Refutation) | Rooted in early Christian apologetics, Aristotle, and Cicero: naming the specific logical or philological error in the attack (false dichotomy between "Sparta or defenselessness", *pluralis majestatis* usurpation, conflating *wzór* [pattern/ideal] with *kopia* [1:1 copy]). |
| **IV** | **Królewska Narada / Rachunek Królewski** (Sovereign Disputation, Luke 14:31–32) | Grounded in Christ's parable of the king weighing 10,000 troops against 20,000 before battle: sober, quantitative demographic, economic, and game-theoretic reckoning that exposes romantic militarist illusions. |
| **V** | **Uczciwa Kontrargumentacja** (Fair-Minded Refutation) | Addressing the strongest, most articulate version of an objection rather than cheap slogans, and testing internal consistency between an interlocutor's public declarations and their policy prescriptions. |

## 2. The Five Doctrinal Pillars of Przymierze Lemiesza (*Doktryna „Oazy Pokoju Europy Środkowej”*)

### Pillar I · Theological & Moral Realism (*Chrystus kontra Wegecjusz*)
- **Scriptural Foundation:** Isaiah 2:4 (*„przekują swe miecze na lemiesze”*), Matthew 5:9 (*„Błogosławieni pokój czyniący, albowiem oni synami Bożymi będą nazwani”*), Polish proverb *„Pokój czyniącym szczęści Bóg”*, Luke 14:31–32 (*Rachunek Królewski*), 1 Samuel 8:11–17 (Samuel's warning against the militarist state enslaving sons and taxing the harvest), and John 10:11–13 (the Good Shepherd vs. the Hireling).
- **Magisterial Foundation:** *Pacem in Terris* (§112, §127) and *Gaudium et Spes* (§78, §79 para. 4): peace is an enterprise of justice (*opus iustitiae*), not a mere balance of terror, and legitimate territorial defense is fundamentally distinct from arms-race ruin or great-power subjugation.
- **Refutation of Pagan Militarism:** We reject the pagan Roman maxim of Vegetius (*Si vis pacem, para bellum*), which bankrupted the Roman Empire through tax slavery and inflation and triggered the catastrophe of 1914 via the security dilemma. When facing powers of vastly greater scale, Christian wisdom commands preemptive diplomacy (*wyprawia poselstwo i prosi o warunki pokoju*, Łk 14, 32).

### Pillar II · Economic Fertility of the Plowshare vs. Sterility of the Sword (*Płodność Lemiesza a Bezpłodność Miecza*)
- **The Plowshare (*Lemiesz*):** Productive civilian capital, family farms, craftsmanship, entrepreneurship, and infrastructure generate real wealth, feed the nation, and sustain demographic renewal.
- **The Sterile Sword:** Tanks, missiles, and stockpiled munitions bought on foreign credit generate zero added value; they consume taxpayer capital, rust in depots, and require costly disposal. As Gen. Dwight D. Eisenhower warned (*Chance for Peace*, 16 April 1953), every gun made and rocket fired is, in the final sense, a theft from those who hunger and are not fed. Following Frédéric Bastiat (*What Is Seen and What Is Not Seen*), transferring national wealth into armaments impoverishes society under the illusion of strength.
- **Illusion of Credit-Bought Sovereignty:** Buying foreign weapons on usurious credit without source codes, spare-parts autonomy, or domestic ammunition chains is not sovereignty—it is the most expensive form of technological and financial vassalage.

### Pillar III · Historical Anti-Sanacja & Moral Asymmetry of Risk (*Lekcja 1939 i Most w Kutach*)
- **The Sanacja "Poland-Sparta" Warning:** In the 1930s, Sanacja elites devoted 35% to nearly 50% of the Polish state budget to the military while the countryside split matches in four and lacked capital for electrification, education, and civilian industry—yet the state still collapsed in September 1939 within two weeks.
- **Demography vs. Barracks:** With a total fertility rate around 1.16, locking tens of thousands of young Poles in barracks instead of families, workshops, and enterprises is a demographic death sentence. Ancient Sparta sacrificed philosophy, commerce, and family life for the barracks, and vanished from history through demographic implosion.
- **Moral Asymmetry of Risk (John 10:11–13):** On the night of 17–18 September 1939, the government that demanded absolute sacrifice from citizens fled across the bridge at Kuty into Romania, leaving millions of Polish families to the Gestapo and NKVD. Like the Gospel Hireling (*Najemnik*), politicians who beat the drums of war hold diplomatic passports and evacuation planes, while ordinary taxpayers stay under the bombs.

### Pillar IV · Active Neutrality & Double Distance (*Od Kleszczy do Pomostu — Oaza Pokoju*)
- **Pattern (*Wzór*), Not 1:1 Copy (*Kopia*):** Przymierze Lemiesza proposes neutrality *na wzór* (as a legal-diplomatic model adapted to Polish conditions), never a mechanical *kopia* of Alpine topography.
- **Game-Theoretic Realism, Not "Neighbour's Mercy":** Neutrality is not begging for mercy or relying on paper promises; it rests on cold game theory, economic calculus, and territorial deterrence (the "hedgehog" principle) so that respecting Poland's inviolable trade and transit status is more profitable to every neighbour than aggression.
- **International & Constitutional Precedents:**
  - **Hague Convention (V) (1907), Arts. 1, 2, 5:** Neutral territory is inviolable; belligerents are forbidden to move troops or munition convoys across it, and a neutral state must not allow such acts on its territory.
  - **Austria (1955 State Treaty & Constitutional Neutrality Law):** Occupied by four powers (including the Red Army) right on the Cold War front line between NATO and the Warsaw Pact, Austria secured the withdrawal of Soviet troops without a single shot through constitutional permanent neutrality (*immerwährende Neutralität*), turning a geopolitical pincer into a prosperous diplomatic bridge (UN, OSCE, OPEC).
  - **Malta (Constitution Art. 1(3)):** An EU member state with neutrality, non-alignment, and prohibition of foreign military bases enshrined directly in its Constitution.
  - **Costa Rica (Constitution Art. 12):** Abolished its standing army in 1948 and became the most stable and prosperous nation in Central America.
  - **Turkmenistan (Constitution Art. 2):** Bordering the Russian sphere, enshrined permanent neutrality recognized unanimously by the UN General Assembly (1995).
- **Double Distance (*Podwójny Dystans*):** Equal strategic distance from rival power blocs. Turning Poland into a forward military bastion provokes the historical Rapallo / Ribbentrop–Molotov reflex (neighbours striking a deal over Poland's head); becoming Central Europe's **Oasis of Peace (*Oaza Pokoju*)** turns the German–Russian pincer into an indispensable trade and diplomatic bridge.

### Pillar V · Civic Subjectivity, Anti-Fear Governance & Sacredness of Life (*Podmiotowość Obywatela i Świętość Życia*)
- **Against *Pluralis Majestatis* Usurpation:** When political elites declare *"We do not need a neutral Poland"*, Przymierze Lemiesza asks: who is *"We"*? The political apparatus, or the Polish family and taxpayer who pays in blood and property?
- **Rejection of Governance by Fear:** Just as emergency fear psychosis was used during COVID-19 to strip liberties and drain livelihoods under the slogan of "supreme safety", war psychosis is used today to impose debt, censorship, and military servitude.
- **Sacredness of Life & Agrarian Stewardship:** Symbolized by the Plowshare (*Lemiesz*), the Two Crowns, and traditional Polish stewardship of land and table, the movement defends the sanctity of human life, family property, food and energy sovereignty, and peaceful Christian civilization.
